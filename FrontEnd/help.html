Frequently Asked Questions

What is RiseFaze? +

RiseFaze is a business simulation game where you can buy, manage, and grow a digital empire of virtual assets. It's a risk-free environment to practice investment strategies and compete with players globally.

What are RFCs? +

RFC (RiseFaze Currency) is the in-game currency. You use RFC to purchase assets, and your assets, in turn, can generate more RFCs. Your total net worth is measured in RFC.

How do I get started? +

Simply sign up for an account! You'll receive starter assets and a sum of RFCs to begin your journey. From there, you can explore the marketplace to acquire new assets and start building your portfolio.

Is this real money or cryptocurrency? +

No. RiseFaze is a simulation and does not involve real money, cryptocurrency, or any blockchain technology. All assets and currencies are purely for entertainment and educational purposes within the game.
