Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 20, 2025

Your privacy is important to me. This policy outlines how RiseFaze collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information I Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up, I collect your email address and a username. Passwords are securely hashed before storage.
  • Gameplay Data: I record your in-game transactions, asset ownership, and net worth to facilitate the game mechanics.

2. Publicly Visible Information

To support the competitive nature of the game, your Username, Net Worth, and Global Rank are public and visible to all users on the Leaderboard.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

I use browser Local Storage to save your authentication token (to keep you logged in) and your interface preferences (e.g., Dark/Light mode). I do not use third-party tracking cookies.

4. Data Security

I implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information. However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.
